How to find freelance clients (with cold email)

A short, practical playbook — the same process this kit is built around.

1. Build a short list of real prospects

Skip job boards for a moment. Look for businesses that already buy the service you sell: agencies, SaaS teams, local firms, newsletters. Log each contact in a tracker so follow-ups do not slip.

2. Send personalised freelance cold email templates

Open with one true observation about their site, product or content. Offer a small, specific next step (audit, sample, 15-minute call). Keep batches small and honour opt-outs.

3. Follow up on a fixed cadence

Most replies come on the 2nd–4th touch. Use a simple Day 0 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 14 schedule, then a polite break-up email so the conversation ends cleanly.

4. Quote with a rate card and a freelance proposal template

When interest appears, send a clear 3-tier rate card and a one-page proposal: problem, outcome, scope, timeline, investment, next step. Make saying yes easy.
