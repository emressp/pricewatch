How a watch works

Sign up, add a URL, pick how closely to look, and choose how often to check.

1

Add a URL

Any public product, pricing, or info page. Optionally limit it to a CSS selector such as .pricing.

2

Choose what matters

Whole page, or price-only mode when you care about prices and priced products rather than surrounding copy.

3

Get the diff

Daily on the free plan, hourly on paid plans. Email summarizes the change. The app keeps the previous and current text.
