Documented moves relevant to this set

Mailchimp:

Mailchimp’s Free marketing plan tightened to 250 contacts and 500 monthly sends, effective 17 Feb 2026 after a mid-January support/community heads-up. Live pricing page now leans hard on “Under 250 contacts? It’s free.” — a classic freemium gate raise that pushes early-stage senders onto Essentials/Standard sooner.

Impact: High for bootstrapped newsletters and side projects still on Free. Competitor battlecards should refresh Brevo/MailerLite/Kit Free comparisons this week.

Linear: Basic $8→$10, Business $14→$16; legacy Plus/Standard retiring

Live pricing extract on 2 Nov 2026 shows Basic at $10 and Business-tier signals at $16 — matching the late-2025 list-price uplift. Separately, Linear is migrating all workspaces off legacy Plus/Standard onto Basic/Business/Enterprise at the next renewal after 1 Feb 2026.

Impact: Medium–High for eng-tool buyers and anyone still on grandfathered Plus/Standard. Expect renewal sticker shock and feature-gap questions (SAML, triage, private teams).

Notion: AI for Work bundled; Business list price now ~$20/user

Notion’s 13 May 2025 ‘AI for Work’ launch bundled Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search, and Research Mode into paid plans. New subscriptions took updated Business/Enterprise pricing immediately; existing customers saw changes at first renewal after 13 Aug 2025. Today’s snapshot no longer surfaces a separate Notion AI add-on — AI is the package, not the upsell.

Impact: High for teams that stacked Notion + separate AI write/search tools. Positioning shift: all-in-one AI workspace vs à-la-carte add-on.

OpenAI ChatGPT: Team → Business packaging; Pro stays premium individual

Through late Aug 2025, ChatGPT Team was repositioned toward a Business offering. Live page extract on 2 Nov 2026 lists Free / Plus / Pro / Business / Enterprise — Team as a named consumer plan is gone from the extract. Pro remains the high individual tier.

Impact: Medium for SMB AI buyers comparing seat SKUs; update “Team” references in decks to Business.
